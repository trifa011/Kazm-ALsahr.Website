About the Artist

Born in Mosul, Iraq, in 1957, Kazem Al-Saher grew up surrounded by music and taught himself to play the oud as a child. He went on to study at the Baghdad Institute of Music, blending classical Arabic maqam traditions with modern orchestration — a signature style that would later define his career.

Over the decades, he has released dozens of albums, written most of his own music, and become one of the best-selling Arabic artists in history, earning the nickname "Caesar of Arabic Song."

Beyond the studio, he is known for weaving poetry into his songwriting, often setting the words of celebrated Arab poets to music, and for using his platform to advocate for peace and cultural exchange.
